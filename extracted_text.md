# Parakkat Nature Hotel & Resorts: Elevate Your Escape in Munnar's Panoramic Paradise

Parakkat Nature Hotel & Resorts in Munnar offers a luxurious escape blending modern amenities with stunning natural beauty. Positioned for panoramic views of lush tea plantations, this premier resort near Chithirapuram is perfect for travelers seeking a unique stay.  
  
With 18 room categories, guests enjoy diverse, opulent accommodations. The resort features a multi-cuisine restaurant serving a variety of delectable dishes, a spa offering rejuvenating treatments, and well-equipped banquet facilities for events. Parakkat Nature Hotel & Resorts is more than a stay; it’s a destination that combines leisure, luxury, and adventure in the heart of Munnar’s picturesque tea country.

### Natural Beauty Abounds

Parakkat Nature Resort is nestled amidst lush green tea plantations, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding hills.

## Frequently asked questions - FAQ’s

#### 1. What are your COVID-19 safety protocols?

We adhere to all safety protocols of COVID-19 on our property to ensure the safety of our guests and our staff. We are committed to delivering the best services. We welcome you to come and have a pleasant stay with us.

  * All linens are sanitised in a high-temperature wash.
  * Guests need to wear face masks in public areas.
  * Our staff wears face masks in public areas.
  * Floors are marked for social distancing.
  * Hand sanitiser is available to guests & staff.
  * We regularly sanitise high-traffic areas.
  * It's mandatory for our staff to regularly wash their hands.
  * We conduct regular temperature checks of our staff.
  * Contactless check-in & check-out.
  * Individually-wrapped toiletries.
  * 24-hour minimum vacancy between check-ins.



#### 2. How do I get to Parakkat Nature Resort from the nearest airport?

Cochin International Airport is the nearest airport to our resort. This would take you about 2 hours and 30 minutes to reach Parakkat Nature Hotel & Resort by car.

#### 3. What are the Check-In times at the Resort?

Check-in at Parakkat Nature Resort is from 2:00 PM, and check-out is until 11:00 AM.

#### 4. How far is Parakkat Nature Resort from Munnar's town centre?

Parakkat Nature Resort is 5 km from the centre of Munnar.

#### 5. What are the breakfast options available at Parakkat Nature Resort?

Guests staying at Parakkat Nature Resort can enjoy our highly-rated breakfast during their stay. We have a guest review score of 8.2.  
  
Breakfast option(s) include:

  * Continental
  * Full English/Irish
  * Vegetarian
  * Vegan
  * Halal
  * Asian
  * American
  * Buffet
  * À la carte



#### 6. What is there to do at Parakkat Nature Resort?

Parakkat Nature Resort offers the following activities/services (charges may apply):

  * Spa - Massage, Spa/Wellness packages, Neck massage, Full-body massage, Head massage, Back massage, Massage chair, Foot massage, Hand massage, Playground
  * Jeep Trekking inside the property.
  * Visit Kerala's First Ever Cave Room.
  * Swimming Pool access.
  * Rope Walking.
  * Rope Cycling.
  * Photoshoot Spots: Over 100+ spots within the property for photography.
  * Camp Fire with Music: A cosy evening activity.
  * Tea Plantation View: Stunning views from the resort.
  * Magical Sunrise and Sunset Views from the resort.
  * Rainwater Dance: Availability depends on the weather.
  * Bird's Eye View Point: Offers a panoramic view of the surrounding landscape.
  * Artificial Waterfall: Operates depending on the weather.
  * Kids Park: A fun area for children to play.
  * Professional Photo Shoot: Available for a charge, conducted by a professional photographer.
