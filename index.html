Information & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning your arrival at our Munnar sanctuary.

1. How do I get to Parakkat Nature Resort from the nearest airport? expand_more
Cochin International Airport (COK) is the nearest airport to our resort. The scenic mountain drive takes approximately 2 hours and 30 minutes (~105 km) via NH 85 through lush pepper vines and rubber plantations. Private resort chauffeur transfers can be pre-arranged with our reservations team.
2. What are the Check-In and Check-Out times at the Resort? expand_more
Check-in at Parakkat Nature Resort is from 2:00 PM, and check-out is until 11:00 AM. Direct bookings via our website or direct phone line receive complimentary early check-in and late check-out privileges, subject to availability on the day.
3. How far is Parakkat Nature Resort from Munnar's town centre? expand_more
Parakkat Nature Resort is situated just 5 km (approximately 15 minutes drive) from the centre of Munnar town at 2nd Mile, Chithirapuram. This provides the perfect balance of easy town access while preserving pure seclusion above the cloud line away from city traffic.
4. What breakfast options are served at Parakkat Nature Resort? expand_more
Guests enjoy our highly rated breakfast buffet (guest review score: 8.2/10). Options include Continental, Full English/Irish, Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Asian, and American spreads, alongside live South Indian dosa, appam, and egg counters served with freshly brewed Munnar tea and estate coffee.
5. What unique adventure activities are available on-site? expand_more
Guests can participate in jeep trekking inside the property, tours of Kerala's first-ever cave room, high-wire rope walking, aerial rope cycling, musical campfires, over 100+ scenic photoshoot spots, seasonal rainwater dance, and artificial waterfalls.
6. Are private pool villas available for couples and families? expand_more
Yes, our collection includes Single Bedroom Pool Villas for honeymooners, Double Bedroom Pool Villas for larger groups or families, and 2 Bedroom Mini Pool Villas, each featuring secluded temperature-controlled private pools overlooking the tea terraces.
