Discover Munnar

Western Ghats • 5,200 Ft. Elevation

Perched Amidst the Rolling Mist & Tea Valleys

The Kashmir of South India

Situated at the confluence of three mountain streams—Muthirapuzha, Nallathanni and Kundala—Munnar is a premier hill station in Kerala. Once the summer resort of the erstwhile British Government in South India, it is marked by vast expanses of tea plantations, colonial bungalows, rivulets, waterfalls and cool weather.

Top Attractions Near Us

Eravikulam National Park

Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and the blooming site of the rare Neelakurinji flowers (which bloom once every 12 years).

Anamudi Peak

The highest peak in South India, standing at an elevation of 2,695 meters. A haven for trekkers and nature enthusiasts.

Mattupetty Dam

Famous for its masonry dam and the beautiful lake, offering thrilling boat rides and surrounded by lush green hills.

Tea Museum

Trace the growth of Munnar's tea estates from rudimentary tea rollers to modern fully automated factories.

How To Reach

flight By Air

The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), approximately 105 km away (a 3-hour scenic drive).

train By Train

Aluva Railway Station is the closest major railhead, located about 110 km from the resort.

directions_car By Road

Munnar is well-connected by National Highway 85. We offer private chauffeured transfers from Kochi upon request.
