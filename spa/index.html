Traditional Ayurvedic Healing

Kerala is the global capital of Ayurveda. Our in-house wellness sanctuary offers bespoke treatments administered by highly trained therapists. Using medicated oils and herbal pastes sourced from the surrounding forests, we provide holistic healing for modern ailments.
